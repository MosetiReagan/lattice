How Does It Work in Real Life?

Tested on standard laptop & desktop CPUs using the Qwen 2.5 open model.

⚡

1. You Ask a Question

Even if a huge document is being summarized, Lattice detects that you are a human sitting at your keyboard waiting for an answer.

⏸️

2. Background Job Pauses in 1ms

Instead of canceling or restarting the background job, Lattice freezes its exact position in RAM. Not a single token of work is lost.

▶️

3. Seamlessly Resumes

The moment your answer is finished, Lattice instantly unfreezes the background job and keeps working as if nothing happened.

How to Use Lattice on Your Computer

Lattice is open-source and compatible with OpenAI clients, Open WebUI, Cursor, and Continue.

Step 1: Install 
pip install lattice-inference

Installs the fast server and CLI tool.

Step 2: Auto-Setup 
lattice init

Checks your CPU, downloads llama.cpp, and tunes RAM.

Step 3: Start Serving 
lattice serve --model model.gguf

Starts an OpenAI-compatible endpoint on port 8000.
